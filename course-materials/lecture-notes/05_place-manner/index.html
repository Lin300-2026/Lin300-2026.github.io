Place

Most articulations have an active and a passive articulator

  • Active: the articulator that moves

  • Passive: the articulator that gets moved to

Most places of articulation are named for the location of the passive articulator.

“Rhotics”

Rhotics aren’t a phonetically coherent set of sounds. Sounds classified as rhotics include

  • alveolar trills

  • uvular trills

  • uvular fricatives

  • velar fricatives

  • alveolar taps

  • retroflex approximants

  • alveolar approximants

  • whatever bunched [ɹ] is

“Rhotics”

As the name would suggest, these are sounds spelled with characters derived from Greek “\(\rho\)”.
